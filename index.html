About Us

What is KitaCraft?

KitaCraft is an educational livelihood platform that equips people with practical crafting and product-development skills that can become a sustainable source of income. Everyone who is committed to learning a skill and starting a small business deserves a chance at economic self-reliance.

🌟 Our Vision

To become a leading livelihood resource, online and in the community, building a self-reliant, creative and economically resilient community where every aspiring entrepreneur can start a sustainable home-based business.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower individuals and local communities through free, accessible digital lessons and hands-on, in-person tutoring in handmade product creation, so learners can set up viable micro-enterprises.

Our Purpose

Why we started this

KitaCraft was created to respond to real problems: unemployment, limited household income, and the lack of accessible entrepreneurial and vocational training. We believe people should be given practical, income-generating skills now, so they can reach financial stability for themselves and their families.

Craft Guides

5 beginner-friendly crafts you can sell

1🧶 Beaded Bracelet

Level: Easy⏱ 15 minutesMaterial cost: about ₱15Sell for: ₱50 to 80

Materials

  • Elastic cord (about 20 cm)
  • Beads
  • Scissors

Steps

  1. Cut the cord to about 20 cm.
  2. Tape one end to the table so the beads don’t slide off.
  3. Thread the beads in your chosen pattern until it fits around your wrist.
  4. Remove the tape and tie two tight knots.
  5. Trim the extra cord and add a dab of clear glue on the knot.

💡 Tip: Stretch the bracelet gently before tying so you know the size is right.

2🔑 Macramé Keychain

Level: Easy⏱ 20 minutesMaterial cost: about ₱20Sell for: ₱60 to 100

Materials

  • Cotton cord or string
  • Keyring
  • Scissors

Steps

  1. Cut 2 cords, each about 50 cm.
  2. Fold both cords in half and loop them onto the keyring.
  3. Take the 4 strands and tie a square knot: left strand over the middle two, right strand under, then reverse.
  4. Repeat the knot 8 to 10 times to form a twisted column.
  5. Tie a knot at the end, trim the cords, and fray the tips for a tassel.

💡 Tip: Keep your knots equally tight so the pattern looks even.

3💌 Handmade Greeting Card

Level: Easy⏱ 20 minutesMaterial cost: about ₱15Sell for: ₱40 to 70

Materials

  • Colored cardboard or cartolina
  • Scissors and glue
  • Markers or colored pens
  • Old magazines or scrap paper for decorations

Steps

  1. Cut the cardboard to 20 x 14 cm and fold it in half.
  2. Cut shapes from scrap paper (flowers, hearts, stars).
  3. Glue them on the front to make a design.
  4. Write a short message with a marker, such as “Happy Birthday!”
  5. Let it dry flat under a book for 10 minutes.

💡 Tip: Make a set of 5 with different themes (birthday, thank you, graduation) and sell them together.

4👜 Painted Tote Bag

Level: Easy to Medium⏱ 1 hour plus dryingMaterial cost: about ₱80Sell for: ₱150 to 250

Materials

  • Plain canvas tote bag
  • Fabric paint
  • Brushes
  • Pencil
  • Cardboard

Steps

  1. Wash and iron the tote bag.
  2. Slip cardboard inside so the paint doesn’t soak through to the back.
  3. Draw your design lightly with a pencil.
  4. Paint with fabric paint, one color at a time. Let each layer dry before adding the next.
  5. Dry for 24 hours, then iron on the reverse side to set the paint.

💡 Tip: Choose simple designs like words, flowers, or patterns. They are faster to make and easier to repeat.

5🕯️ Jar Candle

Level: Medium⏱ 1 hour including coolingMaterial cost: about ₱60Sell for: ₱120 to 180

Materials

  • Candle wax (or old candle bits)
  • Candle wick with metal base
  • Small clean glass jar
  • Stick or pencil
  • Old pot and a smaller container for melting
  • Optional: candle scent or crayon for color

Steps

  1. Glue the wick’s metal base to the center of the jar’s bottom.
  2. Rest a pencil across the jar’s mouth and wrap the wick around it so it stays upright.
  3. Place wax in the small container and set it in the pot of simmering water (never melt wax directly on the flame).
  4. When melted, add scent or a bit of crayon for color and stir.
  5. Pour slowly into the jar, leaving about 1 cm at the top.
  6. Cool for 2 to 3 hours, then trim the wick to 1 cm.

⚠️ Safety: Wax is hot and flammable. Ask an adult to help beginners, and never leave melting wax unattended.
